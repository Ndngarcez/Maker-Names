Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

This policy explains how MakerNames uses cookies and similar browser storage.

Cookie Types

Type Purpose Optional?
Essential session cookies Keep you logged in, protect account access, and support secure downloads. No
Editor preferences Remember local editor choices such as consent choice, UI settings, or saved browser preferences. No, when needed for the service
Analytics Measure traffic, errors, and feature usage through tools such as Google Analytics. Yes
Advertising Show and measure ads through tools such as Google AdSense, prevent ad fraud, and support rewarded downloads. Yes
Payment provider cookies Stripe may use cookies and identifiers when you open checkout or manage a payment. Managed by Stripe

Optional Cookies

Analytics and advertising scripts are designed to load only after you accept optional cookies in the site banner. If you reject optional cookies, login and core editor features should still work, but analytics, ads, and rewarded-ad features may be limited.

Consent Management

The site includes a simple cookie banner for optional analytics and advertising scripts. For users in regions where advertising rules require extra consent controls, such as the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, Google or advertising partners may also require a certified consent management platform.

Managing Cookies

You can accept or reject optional cookies from the banner. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, login and downloads may not work correctly.

Contact

For cookie questions, contact support@makernames.app.
