MakerNames

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MakerNames collects and uses information when you create an account, design files, download packs, contact support, donate, or use free features.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, such as name, email address, password hash, email verification status, account status, and login session data.
  • Editor content, such as names, text, selected fonts, imported fonts, images, SVGs, DXFs, accessories, colors, settings, rendered previews, and generated files.
  • Download and support information, such as download activity, optional donation or payment status, and payment-provider identifiers when you choose to donate.
  • Support information, such as messages you send to us and the email address used to contact us.
  • Technical information, such as IP address, browser/device information, security logs, error logs, and anti-abuse signals.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide the editor, create downloads, manage accounts, send account emails, process support requests, process optional donations, prevent abuse, improve the product, troubleshoot errors, and respond to legal or copyright requests.

Passwords And Security

Passwords are not stored in plain text. We store salted password hashes. Session, verification, and password reset tokens are generated randomly and stored securely where possible. You are responsible for keeping your account email and password safe.

Donations And Payments

Optional donations or payments are processed by Stripe or another payment provider. We do not store full card numbers. Payment providers may process payment, billing, fraud-prevention, tax, and customer information according to their own terms and privacy notices.

Email Providers

We use email providers such as Brevo or SMTP providers to send transactional emails, including account verification, password reset, support, and payment-related messages. Marketing emails, if added later, will include consent and unsubscribe controls where required.

Analytics, Advertising, And Search

With your consent, we may use services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense to measure traffic, improve the service, show ads, and prevent abuse. Optional analytics and advertising scripts are designed to load only after you accept optional cookies. Image search features may use Google services or external search pages when enabled.

Cookies

Essential cookies are used for login, security, and account access. Optional analytics and advertising cookies are used only after consent. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

User Content

You are responsible for the content you upload, import, generate, download, or share. Do not upload fonts, SVGs, images, names, brand references, logos, characters, or third-party artwork unless you have the rights needed to use them. We may remove content that appears to infringe rights or create legal risk.

Retention And Deletion

We keep account data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for security, legal, tax, payment, and abuse-prevention reasons. Generated files and public library files may remain available until removed or replaced. You can request deletion by contacting support@makernames.app.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, object to, restrict, or delete your personal data. Contact support@makernames.app to make a request.

Children

MakerNames is not intended for children to use without a parent, guardian, or teacher. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact support@makernames.app.
