Stonewall Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Stonewall is a puzzle game by NG Studios. This policy explains how the game handles information when you play it.

Information We Collect

Stonewall does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information from players.

Local Game Data

The game may save gameplay progress on your device, such as completed levels, best times, stars, and top player names entered by the player. This data is stored locally on your device and is used only to show progress and records inside the game.

Accounts, Payments, And Advertising

The current version of Stonewall does not include user accounts, subscriptions, in-app purchases, payment processing, advertising SDKs, or analytics SDKs.

Third-Party Platforms

If you download or play Stonewall through a platform such as Google Play, that platform may process information according to its own privacy policy, account settings, device settings, and store requirements.

Children

Stonewall is designed as a simple puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes To This Policy

If future versions add features such as online accounts, ads, analytics, purchases, or cloud saves, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions about Stonewall, contact support@makernames.app.
